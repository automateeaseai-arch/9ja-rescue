What is 9ja Rescue?

9ja Rescue is a third-person action game set in fictional Nigerian landscapes: the open northern savanna with its villages and granite hills, and a southern river valley with rainforest, terraces and a lake. As a rescuer, you travel to a hostile camp, deal with its guards, free the captives held there and lead them safely to the waiting rescue team. Combat is non-graphic.

How you play

  • Arrive on foot, by truck or motorbike, or jump from a cargo plane with a parachute or hang glider.
  • Explore, climb walls and rocks, swim rivers and pick up first-aid kits and ammunition.
  • Sneak past guards or take them on, free the captives, and escort them to the extraction point.
  • Earn Rescue Points and Mission Points, buy equipment, create up to three characters and climb the rankings.

It is designed for phones held sideways, and also works on computers with a keyboard and mouse.

Your account

You can sign in with your email address or with Google. Your account is used only to save your progress online, so it follows you to another phone, and to show your character on the rankings. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
