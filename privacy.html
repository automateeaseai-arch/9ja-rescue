9ja Rescue: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

9ja Rescue is a free 3D rescue game played in the web browser at 9jarescue.app. This page explains what information the game keeps and why.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address, or, if you sign in with Google, the name, email address and profile picture your Google account shares. We use this only to sign you in.
  • Your game progress: Rescue Points, Mission Points, items, characters you create (their names and looks), your current mission and settings.
  • Rankings: if you complete a mission, your character's name and your Mission Points, captives rescued and missions completed appear on the public in-game rankings. Your email is never shown there.

We do not collect your location, contacts, camera, microphone or payment details.

Where it is stored

Accounts and saved progress are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore, data kept in Europe). A copy of your progress and your settings is also kept in your browser on your device so the game works without a connection.

How we use it

Only to run the game: signing you in, saving and restoring your progress, and showing rankings. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not show advertising.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and saved progress, email us from the address you play with and we will remove it within 30 days. Clearing your browser's site data removes the copy on your device.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@9jarescue.app
